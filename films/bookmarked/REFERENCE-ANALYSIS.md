Method: frames pulled at 2 fps across the whole video, the caption band at 8 fps, and 6 fps around every
fast moment. Shot cuts come from ffmpeg scene detection. Loudness was measured with EBU R128 and the
soundtrack was read from a spectrogram.

## Visual language

- **Style.** Flat-vector "explainer" cartoon. Characters have big round heads, white eyes with black pupils, thick
  angled brows, a simple mouth, and a cylinder body with ball hands. There are no outlines; shading is soft
  gradient vignettes only.
- **Two characters, colour-coded.** Character A wears a sage-green henley and has brown hair. His side of every split is **navy blue with
  pinstripes**. Character B wears a charcoal suit and a red tie, has slicked black hair with grey temples, and his side is
  **burgundy with pinstripes**.
- **Camera.** Each shot holds with a very slow push-in (about 3–6 % over the shot). There is no shake, and the only
  "move" is a two-character lean.
- **Captions.** **One word at a time**, white, bold geometric sans (Poppins Bold–like), about 62 px on 1080, with
  a thin dark stroke and a soft drop shadow. Each word sits at about 69 % of the frame height and stays up
  until the next word (so a long word lingers). Punctuation stays on the word ("Lecter?", "Together,", "wins:").
- **Props pop in** with a short scale-overshoot and little gold sparkle stars.
- **Story trick.** One object (a chess piece) is the clue, the weapon and the ending, and it threads through every
  act.

## The formula, in one line

**Hook question with VS card, then a clue object, then the investigation (board, map), then the meeting (door, smile, "I see
you"), then mutual menace (shadow, lean-in), then the shared meal and props, then the TEAM UP turn, then complementary skills,
then the trap and stamp, then the authority stays clueless, then the ritual epilogue (calendar), then the comment-bait poll.**
