# Reference analysis: first-person "In five seconds, a black hole appears above New York" (85.4 s)

576×1024 (9:16), 30 fps, one continuous handheld **first-person** shot with no cuts. A Blender-style city (tall stone
and glass towers, wide avenue, sidewalk with green iron railings, trees in square planters, yellow taxis, street
lamps). The viewer's own **dark-gloved right hand and grey sleeve** are in the lower right of frame, reaching
forward. The camera breathes, sways, turns fast with motion blur, walks, runs, descends stairs and is finally
pulled off the ground into the sky.
